# Code of Conduct

Idea Lab is for everyone: beginners and veterans, builders and dreamers. We want people to feel safe posting half-formed ideas, so how we treat each other matters more than how good an idea is.

## What we expect

- Be kind and assume good intent.
- Critique ideas, not people. Explain why, and suggest something better.
- Welcome newcomers and beginner questions.
- Respect people's time: search before posting and stay on topic.
- Credit other people's ideas and work.

## What's not okay

- Harassment, insults, personal attacks, or discriminatory language of any kind.
- Sexual content, threats of violence, or posting someone's private information.
- Spam, ads, or repeated self-promotion.
- Claiming ownership of an idea posted here, or pressuring people not to build it.
- Sharing other people's confidential or proprietary information.
- Ideas whose main purpose is to harm people, such as malware, scams, or tools for stalking or harassment.

## Reporting

Use **Report content** from the **...** menu on any discussion, comment or issue. If the problem involves a maintainer, or you want to go straight to GitHub, use [GitHub's abuse report form](https://github.com/contact/report-abuse).

## What maintainers may do

Maintainers may edit, hide or delete content, lock threads, and block people from the repository. Usually that means a reminder first, then a temporary block, then a permanent one. Serious cases can skip straight to a block.

## Scope

This applies everywhere in Idea Lab: discussions, issues and pull requests in this repository.
